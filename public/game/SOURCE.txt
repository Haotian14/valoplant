Map artwork, minimap labels, game icons and weapon icons reused from Icarus Strats.
The vision/ files reduce the SVG height model to planar edges. The elevation/
files retain its wall height bands and supports, with ground sampled every
two SVG units. The navigation data retains walkable polygons and portals.
Each polygon's floor height is the mean of its source vertex heights, in
centimetres. Traversal entries add map-specific rope, zipline, drop and teleporter
connections between walkable polygons in canonical attack-side coordinates.
