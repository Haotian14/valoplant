Map artwork, minimap labels, game icons and weapon icons reused from Icarus Strats.
The vision/ files reduce the SVG height model to planar edges. The elevation/
files retain its wall height bands and supports, with ground sampled every
two SVG units. The navigation/ files retain walkable polygons and portals.
